Reads the text of a PDF into the text box. The file stays on this device.

Settings

Quoted speech is read the way the text describes it: whispered, raised or slower, and German or Spanish words and phrases with German or Spanish sounds. Off: everything is read as plain narration. Money, times and dates are read in their spoken form either way (“€2.5 million” as “2.5 million euros”).

An acted voice (frightened, delighted, urgent) made on your own cloud machine instead of on this device. Needs your private studio link, which stays in this browser only. The studio starts when you open the app (about 2 to 5 minutes), so it is usually ready by the time you press Play; until then, and whenever a sentence is late, the voice on this device reads. It stays ready for 45 minutes after the last sentence. Opening the app uses a little of the owner’s Colab units, even if you do not listen. With the switch off, nothing leaves this device, except a link you ask Hearshot to read.

Saves this device’s power and memory by not using its own voice at all; nothing plays until the studio is ready (about 2 to 5 minutes after you open the app), and it needs a connection.

Automatic uses the graphics chip when this browser offers it, otherwise the standard engine. Each needs its own one-time download (about 326 MB for the graphics chip, 163 MB for its smaller experimental model, 177 MB for standard); all stay saved, so you can switch back and forth to compare. If the graphics chip produces faulty audio, Hearshot switches to standard by itself.

Voices: Kokoro (open source, Apache 2.0)
