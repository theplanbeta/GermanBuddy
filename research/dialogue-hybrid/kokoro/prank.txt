“Hello? Can you hear me?”

The voice on the other end was barely a whisper.

“Daniel… I need you to listen very carefully.”

He laughed. “Oh, come on, Sarah! What is it this time? Another one of your ridiculous conspiracy theories?”

“No.” Her voice cracked. “I'm serious.”

Something about the way she said it made him stop smiling.

“Sarah? What's going on?”

Silence.

Then, very quietly, she said, “There's someone in the house.”

Daniel sat upright.

“What do you mean, someone? Did you call the police?”

“I can't. He's standing right outside my door.”

A floorboard creaked.

“Sarah…” Daniel whispered. “Don't move.”

And then—

BANG!

A deafening crash.

“OH MY GOD!” she screamed.

“RUN, SARAH! RUN!”

Footsteps. Heavy breathing. A door slamming shut.

Then… nothing.

Ten seconds passed.

Twenty.

“Sarah?” His voice trembled. “Sarah, please… say something.”

A faint sound came through the phone.

A giggle.

Then another.

Finally, Sarah burst into uncontrollable laughter.

“OH, DANIEL! You should have HEARD yourself!”

He blinked.

“Wait… WHAT?”

“Happy April Fools' Day, you absolute idiot!”

Daniel stared at his phone in disbelief.

“You… you actually—”

He stopped. Inhaled slowly.

“Sarah.”

“Yes?” she replied sweetly.

“I am going to kill you.”

She laughed even harder.

And despite himself, Daniel began to laugh too.

“You're unbelievable,” he murmured.

“I know,” she whispered. “But you love me anyway.”

A pause.

Then, softly—

“Yeah. I really do.”
